    📖 How to Play One Night Werewolf

    🎯 Objective

    Each player gets a secret role. The game lasts one round — night phase followed by day phase. The village votes to exile one player. Werewolves win if no wolf is exiled. Villagers win if a wolf is exiled. The Tanner wins if they are exiled.

    🌙 Night Phase

    Players take turns performing their role's action (in this order):

    • Werewolf — See who your fellow wolves are.
    • Seer — Look at another player's card or two center cards.
    • Robber — Swap your card with another player and look at it.
    • Troublemaker — Swap two other players' cards (don't look).
    • Insomniac — Look at your own final card.
    • Villager / Tanner — No action; click Sleep.

    All night actions are private — only you see the result.

    ☀️ Day Phase

    Everyone discusses openly in chat. Debate who you trust, accuse others, bluff, and form alliances. When ready, the host reveals the votes. The player with the most votes is exiled and their role is revealed!

    🏆 Win Conditions

    • 🐺 Werewolves — Win if no Werewolf is exiled.
    • 🌾 Villagers — Win if a Werewolf is exiled.
    • 🧑‍🌾 Tanner — Wins only if they are exiled.

    Center cards (3 in the middle) are never dealt to any player — they remain a mystery during the game and are only revealed at game over.
